The enquiry form

If you send an enquiry using the form on this site, the name, phone number, email address and message you enter are transmitted to Formspree, a form-handling service, which forwards them to the practice by email and retains a copy in the practice’s Formspree account. Formspree acts as a data processor on the practice’s behalf. Its servers are located outside the European Economic Area, so your details are transferred internationally under the safeguards described in Formspree’s own privacy terms.

The lawful basis for this processing is your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box before sending the form. Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry.

Please do not use the form to send detailed medical information. It is intended for booking and general enquiries. Anything you send about your health travels through a third-party service and by ordinary email, neither of which is a secure channel for clinical information — discuss that at your appointment, or by phone.
