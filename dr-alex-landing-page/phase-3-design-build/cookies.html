What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website. They are used here only to measure how the site is used, so that it can be improved.

No cookies are set unless you agree

When you first visit, you are asked whether to allow analytics cookies. Nothing is stored on your device until you choose “Accept”. If you choose “Decline”, no analytics are loaded and no cookies are set at any point.

This site uses no advertising, marketing or social-media tracking cookies, and shares no data with advertisers.

Cookies used, if you accept

Accepting loads Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, which sets:

CookiePurposeExpires
_ga Distinguishes one visitor from another, so repeat visits are not double-counted 2 years
_ga_<ID> Keeps track of a single visit (a “session”) 2 years

Your IP address is anonymised. Analytics reports how many people visited, which sections they read and whether they arrived from a search engine — never who you are, and nothing about your health or your reason for visiting.

Remembering your choice

Your answer is kept in your browser’s local storage under apc-cookie-consent, so you are not asked again on every page. This is not a cookie, is never sent to this practice or to anyone else, and stays in your browser.
