# Phase 1 — Research & Keyword Summary

**For Dr Mudrakouski's review.** Plain-English summary of the research behind the page. The full
working detail sits in the other files in this folder.

---

## What we looked at

We reviewed the pain clinics competing for the same searches in Dublin, and ran live searches for
each of the treatments you offer to see who currently appears and what is missing.

## Who you are competing with

**Dublin Pain Clinic and Beacon Pain Clinic are the same organisation** — same phone, email and
address, operating two websites. They are the main competitor: consultant-led, insurance-based,
20+ years established. Others include Mater Private, a Dun Laoghaire integrative clinic, and
several physiotherapy and cosmetic clinics offering PRP.

Realistically, we will not outrank an established group on a broad term like "pain clinic Dublin"
quickly. The plan targets where you can genuinely win instead.

## Where you can win

**1. Radiofrequency ablation — the strongest opportunity.**
When we searched for this in Ireland, the results were filled almost entirely with American and
British hospital websites. There is very little Irish content on it. An Irish page, written by a
consultant who performs the procedure, is exactly what those results are missing.

**2. Questions about cost, insurance and referrals.**
People searching what an injection costs in Ireland are currently landing on discussion forums,
because no clinic answers plainly. Your confirmed position — a GP referral is welcome but not
required, self-referred patients accepted, six insurers plus self-pay — answers questions your
competitors leave open.

**3. Consultant-delivered PRP.**
PRP in Ireland is mostly offered by cosmetic and physiotherapy clinics. Very few consultants
appear. Your specialist registration and FIPP accreditation are a genuine and honest point of
difference here, and the page says so.

## What the page targets

Every treatment you listed has its own heading on the page, which is how a single page can appear
for several different searches:

Spinal injections · Shoulder injection · Knee injection · Radiofrequency ablation · Back pain ·
Neck pain · Sciatica · PRP / Platelet Rich Plasma

## One honest limitation

We can see **who you are competing with** and **where the gaps are**. We have not yet measured
**how many people search each term per month** — that needs Google's Keyword Planner, which runs
against a live advertising account. It is worth doing before spending money on any of this, and it
may reorder the priorities above.

## Recommended next step

Add a short questions-and-answers section to the page covering cost, insurance cover, whether a
referral is needed, and typical waiting time. It answers what patients actually ask, and it is the
content most likely to bring in searches you can realistically win.
